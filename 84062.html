Artificial Intelligence

Artificial intelligence (AI) is the simulation of human intelligence processes by computer systems. These processes include learning (the acquisition of information and rules for using the information), reasoning (using rules to reach approximate or definite conclusions) and self-correction.

Machine Learning

Machine learning is an application of artificial intelligence (AI) that provides systems the ability to automatically learn and improve from experience without being explicitly programmed. Machine learning focuses on the development of computer programs that can access data and use it learn for themselves.

Natural Language Processing

Natural language processing (NLP) is a branch of artificial intelligence (AI) that enables computers to understand, interpret, and manipulate human language. NLP combines computational linguistics—rule-based modeling of human language—with statistical, machine learning, and deep learning models.
